Offline regeneration; no LLM or fuzzing was executed.

F6 provenance: F6 is derived from the first actual provider call of each F5 run. It is a paired one-call checkpoint, not an independent recovery run. Missing first-call or first-campaign artifacts are marked CANCELLED rather than guessed.
